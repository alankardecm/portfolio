## Prompt
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: exclusive 16:9 landscape hero art for AM Consultoria, a high value Brazilian data, AI and operational strategy consulting boutique. Create a sophisticated cinematic industrial design sculpture: an imposing central smoked glass and satin titanium rectangular architectural core, surrounded by three huge finely machined elliptical metallic orbit rings at different angles, suspended above a reflective dark graphite plinth. Thin cyan light guides travel through the rings into the core, suggesting many operations converging into one intelligence. The sculpture occupies the right 65% of the composition, the left 35% fades into almost black, usable negative space for website text. Museum level object photography meets highly crafted photorealistic 3D industrial rendering. Materials have real brushed metal grain, beveled glass edges, highly controlled reflections, tiny precision details, deep atmospheric shadow and cyan rim lighting. Restrained black, gunmetal, silver, icy cyan with a tiny warm champagne reflection. Low three-quarter camera, sculptural scale, dramatic depth, extremely elegant, confident, premium B2B. Full bleed edge to edge. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, computer screens, decorative particles, brains, robots, stock business people, vague nebulas, or generic glowing sphere. This must feel like a commissioned design object photographed for an international strategic technology firm, not a simple icon or template.

## Uso
Arte de identidade conceitual. Não apresentar como fotografia de instalação ou equipamento de cliente. Não há rosto sintético do fundador. Textos e diagramas são construídos em HTML/CSS para manter nitidez, acessibilidade e adaptação às telas.
